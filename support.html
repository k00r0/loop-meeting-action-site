Support — Loop - Meeting Actions

Loop is a privacy-first meeting workflow agent: Record → Transcribe on-device → Actions → Weekly Plan. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Getting started

  1. Open Record, confirm you have informed everyone, tap Start Recording.
  2. Tap Stop & Transcribe. Actions appear in Actions tab and Today tab.
  3. For phone calls: use iPhone's built-in Call Recording (iOS 18.1+), then Share → Loop to import, or use speakerphone + in-app announcement.

Free vs Pro

Free: 3 meetings / month transcribed.
Pro Monthly $14.99/mo, Pro Yearly $99.99/yr (7-day trial). Restore via Settings → Restore Purchases.

Troubleshooting

  • No transcript: ensure the Whisper model is downloaded in Settings → On-device Models (WiFi, once), then retry. Airplane Mode works after download.
  • Microphone: Settings app → Loop → Microphone must be On.
  • Model download fails: check WiFi, retry once.

Privacy: Data Not Collected. No account. No cloud. See Privacy Policy.

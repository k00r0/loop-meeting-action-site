Privacy Policy — Loop - Meeting Actions

Data Not Collected. Loop does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or any third party.

  • Audio, transcripts, summaries, action items, and weekly plans stay in the app's sandbox on your iPhone.
  • Transcription and action extraction run on-device. No audio or transcript is sent to any server.
  • No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no Firebase, no AdMob.
  • The only network use is an optional, user-initiated one-time download of the on-device Whisper model from Hugging Face. No audio or personal data is uploaded during that download.
  • Export happens only when you tap Export / Share Sheet. You choose where it goes (Files, Google Docs, Reminders, Calendar).
  • If you tap Export to Google Docs, Loop sends only the meeting content you chose to YOUR Google account, using YOUR sign-in (drive.file scope — Loop can only see files it creates). Same for Notion / Todoist: only your chosen content goes to your own connected account. The Loop developer receives nothing.
  • If you enable iCloud backup, Apple may back up the app's local files per your device settings. Loop itself does not sync to iCloud.

Recording consent: You must inform other participants before recording. In California and other all-party-consent jurisdictions, recording without telling them is illegal even if the recording stays on your phone. The app shows a consent toggle and can play an audible announcement.

Contact: See Support page. No data requests are needed because we hold no data about you.
